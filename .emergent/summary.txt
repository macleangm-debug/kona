<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user requested the implementation of a series of features for their Kona video streaming platform. The key requirements included:
- A redesigned, animated, and clickable pre-splash screen.
- An adaptive video player that can handle both vertical (for stories) and horizontal (for standard content) videos gracefully.
- Gesture controls in the video player, specifically double-tap to like and horizontal swipes to navigate between episodes.
- A Stories feature to showcase short, vertical videos, with a rule that the first episode of any series must be a vertical Story.
- A complete Business/Advertiser Portal for businesses to register, create, and manage ad campaigns.
- An ad system with a pre-pay wallet model, where advertisers top up a balance and budgets are reserved from this wallet for campaigns.
- Integration of the ad system into the main video player to serve pre-roll and mid-roll ads from approved campaigns.
- A visually appealing coin animation to provide positive feedback when a user earns rewards or purchases in-app currency.
- Geo-location tracking to identify and store the user's country based on their IP address during registration and login.
