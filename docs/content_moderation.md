# Kona - Content Moderation Guidelines

## Overview

This document establishes content moderation policies to ensure Kona provides a safe, enjoyable platform for all users while respecting local cultural norms and legal requirements.

---

## 1. Content Categories

### Allowed Content

✅ **Entertainment**
- Drama, romance, comedy, thriller, action
- Cultural and educational content
- Music videos and performances
- Documentary content
- User-generated creative content (within guidelines)

✅ **Age-Appropriate Content**
- Family-friendly content (no age restriction)
- Teen content (13+) with appropriate warnings
- Mature content (18+) with age verification

### Prohibited Content

❌ **Absolutely Prohibited**
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- Content promoting terrorism or violent extremism
- Content inciting violence against individuals or groups
- Human trafficking or exploitation content

❌ **Restricted Content**
- Explicit pornography
- Extreme graphic violence
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide
- Content promoting illegal drug use
- Copyright-infringing material

---

## 2. Content Rating System

### Rating Categories

| Rating | Age | Description | Content Allowed |
|--------|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| **G** | All ages | General audience | No restrictions |
| **PG** | 7+ | Parental guidance suggested | Mild language, mild violence |
| **T** | 13+ | Teen | Moderate language, romance, action |
| **M** | 18+ | Mature | Strong language, violence, adult themes |
| **R** | 18+ (Restricted) | Adult only | Explicit content (where legal) |

### Rating Criteria

**Language:**
- G: No profanity
- PG: Mild expletives (damn, hell)
- T: Moderate profanity (occasional strong language)
- M: Strong profanity permitted
- R: No restrictions

**Violence:**
- G: No violence or very mild cartoon violence
- PG: Mild action, no blood
- T: Action violence, minimal blood
- M: Graphic violence, blood
- R: Extreme violence (where legal)

**Sexual Content:**
- G: None
- PG: Mild romance (kissing)
- T: Moderate romance, implied intimacy
- M: Sexual themes, partial nudity
- R: Explicit content (where legal)

**Substance Use:**
- G: None
- PG: None
- T: Brief references
- M: Depicted but not glorified
- R: No restrictions

## 3. Regional Considerations

### Kenya
- **Legal Framework:** Kenya Film Classification Board (KFCB)
- **Restricted:** Explicit homosexual content, extreme violence
- **Required:** Age gates for 18+ content
- **Action:** Comply with KFCB ratings

### Nigeria
- **Legal Framework:** National Film and Video Censors Board (NFVCB)
- **Restricted:** Content offensive to religious sensibilities
- **Required:** NFVCB classification for locally produced content
- **Action:** Work with local classification board

### South Africa
- **Legal Framework:** Film and Publication Board (FPB)
- **Restricted:** Propaganda for war, incitement to violence
- **Required:** FPB registration for VOD services
- **Action:** Obtain FPB registration

### Pan-African General
- **Respect:** Local cultural and religious sensitivities
- **Consider:** Tribal and ethnic sensitivities
- **Avoid:** Political content that could incite unrest

## 4. Moderation Process

### Pre-Publication Review

**Creator-Uploaded Content:**

```
Step 1: Automated Scan
├── AI content analysis
├── Copyright detection (Content ID)
├── CSAM detection (PhotoDNA)
└── Metadata validation

Step 2: Human Review (if flagged)
├── Content specialist review
├── Rating assignment
├── Regional compliance check
└── Approval/Rejection decision

Step 3: Publication
├── Content published with rating
├── Age gates applied
└── Regional restrictions set
```

**Turnaround Times:**
| Queue Priority | Response Time |
|----------------|---------------|
| Standard | 48 hours |
| Expedited (paid) | 12 hours |
| Emergency | 4 hours |

### User-Reported Content

**Report Categories:**
1. Illegal content (CSAM, terrorism)
2. Harassment/bullying
3. Hate speech
4. Copyright violation
5. Misinformation
6. Spam
7. Other policy violation

**Response Times:**
| Category | Response Time | Action Required |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| CSAM | Immediate | Remove + report to authorities |
| Terrorism | 1 hour | Remove + law enforcement |
| Harassment | 4 hours | Review + action |
| Copyright | 24 hours | DMCA process |

## 5. Enforcement Actions

### Content Actions

| Violation Severity | Action |
|-------------------|--------|
| Minor | Warning + education |
| Moderate | Content removal |
| Severe | Content removal + account warning |
| Critical | Immediate removal + account suspension |

### Account Actions

| Violation Count | Action |
|-----------------|--------|
| 1st violation | Warning |
| 2nd violation | 7-day feature restriction |
| 3rd violation | 30-day suspension |
| 4th violation | Permanent ban |
| Severe (single) | Immediate permanent ban |

### Appeal Process

1. **Submit Appeal:** Within 30 days via support@kona.app
2. **Review:** Different moderator reviews within 5 business days
3. **Decision:** User notified of final decision
4. **Escalation:** Appeals board for disputed decisions

## 7. Automated Moderation

### AI Systems in Use

| System | Purpose | Accuracy |
|--------|---------|----------|
| PhotoDNA | CSAM detection | 99.9% |
| Video AI | Violence/nudity detection | 94% |
| Audio AI | Hate speech detection | 89% |
| Text AI | Comment moderation | 92% |
| Copyright | Content ID matching | 97% |

### Human Override

All automated decisions can be overridden by human moderators. Automated systems are configured for:
- **High confidence (>95%):** Auto-action with review queue
- **Medium confidence (80-95%):** Flag for human review
- **Low confidence (<80%):** Pass through

## 9. Creator Guidelines

### Before You Upload

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Content is original or properly licensed
- [ ] No copyrighted music without permission
- [ ] Appropriate age rating selected
- [ ] No prohibited content
- [ ] Proper content warnings added

### Content Best Practices

**Do:**
- Create engaging, original stories
- Use appropriate age ratings
- Add content warnings for sensitive material
- Respond to moderation requests promptly
- Respect intellectual property

**Don't:**
- Include prohibited content
- Mislead with thumbnails/titles
- Bypass age restrictions
- Re-upload removed content
- Harass other users or creators

## 10. Legal Compliance

### DMCA/Copyright Process

1. **Receive Notice:** Valid DMCA notice submitted
2. **Remove Content:** Within 24 hours
3. **Notify Creator:** Counter-notice option
4. **Counter-Notice:** Creator disputes (14-day wait)
5. **Restore/Litigate:** Based on response

### Government Requests

**Process:**
1. Valid legal request received
2. Legal team review
3. Narrowest possible response
4. User notification (where legally permitted)
5. Transparency report inclusion

**Request Types:**
- Content removal orders
- User data requests
- Emergency disclosures
- Preservation requests
