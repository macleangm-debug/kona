## 💰 Revenue Share Model

### Tier Structure

| Tier | Requirements | Revenue Share | Benefits |
|------|--------------|---------------|----------|
| **New** | First series | 60% | Basic analytics, support |
| **Verified** | 1M+ views, 4.0+ rating | 65% | Priority featuring |
| **Partner** | 5M+ views, 4.3+ rating | 70% | Advance payments option |
| **Exclusive** | Top 10 creators | 75% + advance | Co-marketing, production support |

### Revenue Calculation

```
Creator Earnings Formula:

Episode Revenue = Unlocks × Coin Value × Creator Share

Where:
├── Unlocks = Number of times episode unlocked
├── Coin Value = $0.01 per coin (5 coins = $0.05)
├── Creator Share = 60-75% based on tier

Example (Verified Creator):
├── Episode unlocked 10,000 times
├── Revenue = 10,000 × $0.05 × 65%
├── Creator Earnings = $325 per episode
└── 25-episode series = $8,125 total
```

## 📚 Content Guidelines

### Quality Standards

**Video Requirements:**
| Attribute | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Resolution | 1080p | 4K |
| Frame Rate | 24fps | 30fps |
| Bitrate | 5 Mbps | 10 Mbps |
| Audio | 128kbps | 256kbps |

**Episode Format:**
```
Recommended Structure:
├── Length: 2-4 minutes optimal
├── Hook: First 10 seconds crucial
├── Pacing: Quick scene changes
├── Ending: Cliffhanger for next episode
└── Series: 20-40 episodes ideal
```

### Prohibited Content

**Zero Tolerance:**
- ❌ Child exploitation
- ❌ Real violence/gore
- ❌ Hate speech
- ❌ Terrorism content

**Restricted:**
- ⚠️ Adult themes (18+ rating)
- ⚠️ Strong language (rating required)
- ⚠️ Drug references (context matters)
- ⚠️ Violence (stylized OK)

### Content Moderation

```
Review Process:
1. Automated scan (AI)
2. Manual review (first upload)
3. Spot checks (ongoing)
4. User reports (reactive)

Violations:
├── Warning: First minor offense
├── Content removal: Significant violation
├── Account suspension: Repeated violations
└── Termination: Severe violations
```
