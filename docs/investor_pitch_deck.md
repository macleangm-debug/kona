# Kona - Investor Pitch Deck

## 🎬 The Opportunity

### Problem
- 500M+ Africans lack access to quality, affordable entertainment
- Existing platforms (Netflix, YouTube) are expensive or don't cater to local tastes
- Mobile-first generation needs bite-sized content that fits their lifestyle

### Solution: Kona
A mobile-first mini-series streaming platform with a coin-based economy, designed for African and emerging markets.

## 🏆 Competitive Advantage

### Why Kona Wins

1. **Mobile-First Design**
   - Vertical video format optimized for smartphones
   - Works on 2G/3G networks
   - Offline download capability

2. **Local Payment Integration**
   - M-Pesa, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money
   - No credit card required

3. **Coin-Based Economy**
   - Micro-transactions ($0.10-0.50 per episode)
   - Gamification increases engagement

4. **Creator Partnership Model**
   - 60-70% revenue share for creators
   - Built-in audience for local storytellers

5. **Viral Referral System**
   - User-to-user growth with coin incentives
   - Milestone rewards drive organic growth
