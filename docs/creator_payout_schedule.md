# Kona - Creator Payout Schedule

## Overview

This document outlines the payment terms, schedules, and processes for content creators on the Kona platform.

---

## 1. Revenue Share Structure

### Standard Revenue Share

| Creator Tier | Revenue Share | Requirements |
|--------------|---------------|--------------|
| **New Creator** | 60% | Default for all new creators |
| **Bronze** | 62% | 10,000 views, 3 months |
| **Silver** | 65% | 50,000 views, 6 months |
| **Gold** | 68% | 200,000 views, 12 months |
| **Platinum** | 70% | 1M views, exclusive contract |

### What Counts as Revenue

✅ **Included:**
- Coin purchases used to unlock your episodes
- Subscription revenue attributed to your content
- Ad revenue from your content (when applicable)
- Tips and gifts from viewers

❌ **Excluded:**
- Referral bonuses (creator's own earnings)
- Platform operational fees
- Payment processing fees

## 2. Payment Schedule

### Regular Payout Schedule

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1st-28th | Earnings accumulate |
| 1st of month | Earnings period closes |
| 5th of month | Earnings calculated & verified |
| 10th of month | Payout initiated |
| 15th of month | Funds arrive (bank/mobile money) |

### Payment Timeline

```
Month 1: Content Published → Earnings Accumulate
Month 2, Day 1: Earnings Period Closes
Month 2, Day 5: Verification Complete
Month 2, Day 10: Payout Initiated
Month 2, Day 15: Funds Received
```

**Example:**
- Content earns revenue: January 1-31
- Payout processed: February 10
- Funds received: February 15

### Minimum Payout Threshold

| Payment Method | Minimum Threshold |
|----------------|-------------------|
| M-Pesa (Kenya) | KES 500 (~$5) |
| MTN Mobile Money | GHS 50 (~$5) |
| Bank Transfer | $25 |
| PayPal | $50 |

*Earnings below threshold roll over to next month*

### Tax Responsibilities

**Kona's Responsibility:**
- Issue earnings statements
- Report to tax authorities (where required)
- Withhold taxes (where legally mandated)

**Creator's Responsibility:**
- Report income to local tax authorities
- Pay applicable income taxes
- Maintain accurate records

### Tax Documentation

| Document | Frequency | Purpose |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Monthly Statement | Monthly | Earnings summary |

## 8. Content Advance Program

### Eligibility

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| Account age | 6 months |
| Total earnings | $1,000 |
| Content published | 20 episodes |
| Good standing | No violations |

### Advance Terms

- **Maximum advance:** 3x monthly average earnings
- **Repayment:** Deducted from future earnings (50% of each payout)
- **Interest:** 0% for first advance, 5% for subsequent
- **Frequency:** One advance per quarter

### Application Process

1. Apply via Creator Portal
2. Review (3 business days)
3. Agreement signed digitally
4. Funds disbursed (5 business days)

## 9. Account Termination & Final Payout

### Creator-Initiated

**Process:**
1. Submit termination request (30-day notice)
2. Content remains live during notice period
3. Final earnings calculated
4. Payout within 30 days of termination

### Platform-Initiated

**Grounds:**
- Repeated policy violations
- Fraudulent activity
- Legal requirements

**Payout Policy:**
- Legitimate earnings paid out
- Earnings from violating content forfeited
- Appeals available within 14 days

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will I receive my first payment?**
A: First payment is processed in the month following your first earnings. Example: Earnings in January → Paid February 15.

**Q: Why was my payment delayed?**
A: Common reasons include bank holidays, verification requirements, or technical issues. Check Creator Portal for status.

**Q: Can I change my payment method?**
A: Yes, update payment details in Creator Portal. Changes take effect next payout cycle.

**Q: What happens if I don't reach the minimum threshold?**
A: Earnings roll over to the next month until threshold is met.

**Q: How do I get a higher revenue share?**
A: Progress through creator tiers by growing your viewership and maintaining good standing.

**Q: Are there any fees deducted?**
A: Payment processing fees (typically 2-3%) are deducted. No platform fees on earnings.
