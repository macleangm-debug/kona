# Kona Content Publishing Guide

## Overview
This guide explains how to publish your series so it becomes visible to all viewers on the platform.

## Publishing Workflow

### 1. Create Your Series
1. Go to **Creator Portal** → **My Series**
2. Click **Create New Series**
3. Fill in series details (title, description, genre, thumbnail)
4. Your series will be in "Pending Review" status initially

### 2. Add Episodes
1. Click on your series to open it
2. Click **Add Episodes** button
3. Upload video files (supported formats: MP4, WebM, MOV)
4. Videos are automatically uploaded to **Bunny.net CDN** for global streaming
5. Wait for encoding to complete (status: "Queued" → "Processing" → "Ready")

**Important:** 
- Only episodes with "Ready" status will be published to viewers
- Encoding typically takes 2-5 minutes depending on video length
- You can preview your video once encoding is complete

### Preview Your Videos
1. Click on any episode to open the editor
2. If the video has been uploaded, click **Preview Video**
3. A video player will open showing your content
4. If still processing, you'll see a "Video is processing" message

### 3. Organize Seasons
- Episodes are automatically grouped into seasons
- Drag and drop to reorder episodes
- Use **Bulk Edit** to move multiple episodes between seasons
- Season 1, Episode 1 is always free (pilot episode)

### 4. Publish Your Series
1. Ensure you have at least 1 episode with "Ready" status
2. Click the green **Publish Series** button
3. Your series will be visible to all viewers immediately

## What Happens During Publishing

When you click "Publish":
1. Your series is copied from creator workspace to the public catalog
2. All "Ready" episodes are made available for streaming
3. Episode 1 of Season 1 is automatically marked as free
4. Viewers can find your series on Home and Discover pages

## Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending Review | Series created, awaiting publish |
| Approved | Admin-approved (for moderated content) |
| Published | Live and visible to all viewers |
| Processing | Video is being encoded by CDN |
| Ready | Video is ready for streaming |

## Why Videos May Not Show

If your published videos aren't showing to viewers:

1. **Episode not "Ready"**: Check encoding status - only "Ready" episodes are published
2. **Series not "Published"**: Click the Publish button
3. **Cache**: Public catalog may take 1-2 minutes to update
4. **Video URL missing**: Re-upload the video if encoding failed

## Re-Publishing After Changes

After adding new episodes to a published series:
1. Upload and wait for "Ready" status
2. Click **Publish Series** again to update the public catalog
3. New episodes will be available to viewers

## Troubleshooting

### "No episodes" error when publishing
- Upload at least 1 video
- Wait for encoding to complete (status: "Ready")

### Video not playing for viewers
- Check if video has a valid Bunny.net URL
- Re-upload if encoding failed
- Contact support if issue persists

### Series not appearing in search
- Ensure series is "Published" status
- Check title and description for searchable keywords
- Allow 5-10 minutes for search index update
