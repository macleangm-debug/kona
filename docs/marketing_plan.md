## 🚀 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Month 1-3)

#### Channel Mix

| Channel | Budget % | Target Users | CAC Target |
|---------|----------|--------------|------------|
| Influencer Marketing | 40% | 40,000 | $0.50 |
| Social Media Ads | 30% | 30,000 | $0.50 |
| App Store Optimization | 10% | 15,000 | $0.10 |
| PR & Press | 10% | 10,000 | $0.25 |
| Referral Program | 10% | 5,000 | $0.30 |

#### Influencer Strategy

**Tier 1: Macro Influencers (500K+ followers)**
- Budget: $10,000
- Target: 5 influencers
- Deliverables: 2 posts + 3 stories each
- Expected Reach: 5M impressions

**Tier 2: Mid-tier Influencers (50K-500K)**
- Budget: $5,000
- Target: 20 influencers
- Deliverables: 1 post + 2 stories each
- Expected Reach: 3M impressions

**Tier 3: Micro Influencers (10K-50K)**
- Budget: $3,000
- Target: 50 influencers
- Deliverables: 1 post + story
- Expected Reach: 1.5M impressions

#### Social Media Advertising

**Facebook/Instagram**
- Daily Budget: $300
- Targeting: Interest-based (drama, romance, entertainment)
- Ad Formats: Video ads (15-30 sec clips from series)
- Optimization: App installs → Episode views

**TikTok**
- Daily Budget: $150
- Targeting: 18-34, entertainment interests
- Ad Formats: In-feed video, spark ads
- Content: Behind-the-scenes, series teasers

**Twitter/X**
- Daily Budget: $50
- Targeting: Entertainment, TV show discussions
- Ad Formats: Video cards, conversation ads

#### PR & Press Strategy

**Launch Press Kit Contents:**
- Press release
- Founder story
- Platform screenshots
- Usage statistics
- High-res logos
- B-roll footage

**Target Publications:**
- TechCrunch (tech angle)
- Variety (entertainment)
- African tech blogs
- Local entertainment news

### Phase 2: Growth (Month 4-9)

#### Viral Loops Implementation

**1. Referral Program Optimization**
```
Current: Refer friend → Both get 30 coins
Enhanced:
├── Level 1: 1-5 referrals → 30 coins each
├── Level 2: 6-15 referrals → 40 coins each
├── Level 3: 16-30 referrals → 50 coins each
└── Level 4: 31+ referrals → 60 coins + VIP day
```

**2. Social Sharing Mechanics**
- Share episode clip → Earn 5 coins
- Share series → Earn 10 coins
- Viral clip reaches 1000 views → Creator bonus

**3. Watch Party Feature**
- Invite friends to watch together
- Host earns coins for each participant
- Group chat during viewing

#### Content Marketing

**YouTube Channel**
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Creator interviews
- Series previews
- Watch time: Target 100K hours/month

**Blog/SEO**
- Episode recaps
- Series reviews
- Industry news
- Target: 50K organic visits/month

**Email Marketing**
- Welcome series (5 emails)
- Weekly new content digest
- Personalized recommendations
- Re-engagement campaigns
