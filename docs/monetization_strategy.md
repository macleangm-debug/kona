### Coin Spending

| Action | Coins Required |
|--------|----------------|
| Unlock Episode | 5 coins |
| Premium Episode | 10 coins |
| Early Access Episode | 15 coins |
| Download Episode (VIP) | 0 coins |

### Coin Economy Balance

**Goal:** Users should be able to watch ~2-3 episodes/day for free through earning, but need to purchase for binge sessions.

```
Daily Coin Earnings (Active User):
├── Daily Login: 10 coins
├── Lucky Spin (avg): 15 coins
├── Daily Mission: 10 coins
└── Total: ~35 coins/day

Episodes Watchable: 35 ÷ 5 = 7 episodes/day

Binge Session (20 episodes):
├── Free coins: 7 episodes
├── Need to buy: 13 episodes = 65 coins
└── Minimum purchase: $0.99 (100 coins)
```

## 📈 Conversion Optimization

### Funnel Targets

| Stage | Current | Target | Tactics |
|-------|---------|--------|---------|
| Install → Register | 60% | 80% | Simplified onboarding |
| Register → Watch Ep1 | 70% | 90% | Auto-play first episode |
| Watch Ep1 → Watch Ep2 | 40% | 60% | Cliffhanger endings |
| Watch Ep2 → Purchase | 10% | 20% | "Almost there" prompts |

### Paywall Optimization

**"Almost There" Prompt Strategy:**
```
Trigger: User has <10 coins, tries to unlock episode
Message: "You're only 3 coins away! 
         Complete today's spin for a chance to earn more,
         or get 100 coins for just $0.99"
Options: [Spin Wheel] [Buy Coins]
```

### Subscription Upsell Moments

1. **After 5th coin purchase:** "Save 20% with a subscription"
2. **When downloading:** "Get unlimited downloads with Premium"
3. **After watching 10 episodes:** "Unlock early access with VIP"
4. **On anniversary:** "Special offer: 50% off first month"

## 🎯 Pricing Experiments

### A/B Tests to Run

| Test | Hypothesis | Metric |
|------|------------|--------|
| Coin prices | Lower starter price increases conversion | First purchase rate |
| Subscription annual | Annual discount increases LTV | Subscription revenue |
| Episode pricing | Variable pricing by popularity | Episodes unlocked |
| Free coins | More free coins increases engagement | DAU, then conversion |

### Price Elasticity Testing

```
Test Matrix:
├── Starter Package: $0.99 vs $1.49 vs $1.99
├── Popular Package: $2.49 vs $2.99 vs $3.49
└── Subscription: $4.99 vs $5.99 vs $6.99

Duration: 2 weeks each
Sample: 10% of new users
```

## 💸 Revenue Leakage Prevention

### Fraud Prevention

- **Velocity checks:** Max 3 purchases/hour
- **Device fingerprinting:** Flag suspicious devices
- **Referral abuse:** Verify unique devices
- **Chargeback monitoring:** Block repeat offenders

### Coin Economy Controls

- **Inflation control:** Monitor coins in circulation
- **Earning caps:** Max 100 free coins/day
- **Expiration:** Earned coins expire after 90 days (optional)
