# Kona Customer Support Playbook
## Support Excellence for 10M+ Users

---

## 🎯 Support Vision

**Mission:** Resolve user issues quickly and empathetically, turning problems into loyalty.

**Goals:**
- First Response Time: <2 hours
- Resolution Time: <24 hours
- Customer Satisfaction: >90%
- First Contact Resolution: >70%

---

## 📊 Support Tiers

### Tier Structure

| Tier | Scope | Response Time | Handle By |
|------|-------|---------------|-----------|
| **Tier 1** | Common issues, FAQs | <2 hours | Support agents |
| **Tier 2** | Technical issues, billing | <8 hours | Senior agents |
| **Tier 3** | Complex bugs, escalations | <24 hours | Engineering |
| **VIP** | Premium subscribers | <1 hour | Dedicated team |

### Escalation Matrix

```
Issue Severity Levels:

P0 - Critical (Immediate)
├── Platform-wide outage
├── Payment system down
├── Security breach
└── Escalate to: CTO + On-call

P1 - High (Within 4 hours)
├── Feature completely broken
├── Billing errors affecting many users
├── Content not loading for segment
└── Escalate to: Engineering Lead

P2 - Medium (Within 24 hours)
├── Individual account issues
├── Single transaction problems
├── Minor feature bugs
└── Escalate to: Tier 2

P3 - Low (Within 48 hours)
├── Feature requests
├── General inquiries
├── Feedback
└── Handle at: Tier 1
```

## 📝 Common Issues & Responses

### 1. Account Issues

#### Can't Login

**Possible Causes:**
- Wrong password
- Account doesn't exist
- Account suspended

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm sorry you're having trouble logging in. Let me help you get back into your account!

Please try these steps:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen
2. Enter your registered email: [email]
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the reset link
4. Create a new password

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, please reply and I'll investigate further.

Best,
[Agent Name]
Kona Support
```

#### Account Suspended

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Your account has been suspended due to [reason: ToS violation / suspicious activity / payment issue].

[If ToS violation:]
Our records show [specific violation]. As per our Terms of Service, this resulted in account suspension.

[If payment issue:]
We detected a chargeback or payment dispute on your account. Please resolve this with your payment provider.

To appeal this decision, please reply with:
1. Explanation of the situation
2. Any relevant documentation

We'll review within 48 hours.

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

### 2. Payment Issues

#### Coins Not Credited

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I understand how frustrating it is when coins don't appear after purchase. Let me look into this right away.

I found your transaction:
- Transaction ID: [ID]
- Amount: [Amount]
- Date: [Date]

[If payment successful:]
Great news! I've verified the payment was successful. Your coins have now been credited. Please refresh the app to see your updated balance of [X] coins.

[If payment failed:]
It looks like the payment didn't complete successfully. Your bank may have a pending charge that will be released within 3-5 business days. Please try the purchase again.

Let me know if you need anything else!

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

#### Refund Request

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out about a refund.

I see you purchased [product] on [date] for [amount].

[If within refund window - 14 days EU, 7 days others:]
I've processed your refund of [amount]. You should see this back in your account within 5-10 business days.

[If coins already used:]
I see that [X] coins from this purchase have already been used to unlock episodes. I can offer a partial refund of [amount] for the unused coins.

[If outside refund window:]
Unfortunately, this purchase is outside our refund window of [X] days. However, I'd like to help make this right. Can you tell me more about why you're requesting a refund?

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

### 3. Content Issues

#### Episode Won't Play

**Troubleshooting Steps:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Update app to latest version
3. Clear app cache
4. Try different network (WiFi vs data)
5. Reinstall app

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm sorry the episode isn't playing. Let's get this sorted!

Please try these steps:
1. Close and reopen the Kona app
2. Check your internet connection
3. If on WiFi, try switching to mobile data (or vice versa)
4. Update to the latest app version from [App Store/Play Store]
5. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > Kona > Clear Cache)

If the issue persists, please let me know:
- Which series and episode?
- What error message do you see?
- What device and OS version are you using?

I'll have our team investigate immediately.

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

#### Content Complaint

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We take content quality and community standards seriously.

[If quality issue:]
I've forwarded your feedback to our content team. They'll review [series name] and work with the creator to address the issues you mentioned.

[If offensive content:]
I've flagged this content for immediate review. Our Trust & Safety team will investigate within 24 hours and take appropriate action.

We appreciate you helping us maintain a great experience for all viewers.

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

### 4. Subscription Issues

#### Cancel Subscription

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm sorry to see you go! I can help you cancel your subscription.

Before I process this, I wanted to let you know:
- Your subscription is active until [date]
- You'll lose access to: [benefits list]
- You have [X] unused coins that won't expire

[If eligible for offer:]
Would you be interested in staying at a 50% discount for the next month? Just reply "YES" and I'll apply it.

[To proceed with cancellation:]
Reply "CANCEL" and I'll process this immediately.

Best,
[Agent Name]
```

### 5. Technical Issues

#### App Crashing

**Response Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm sorry the app keeps crashing. Let's fix this!

Please try these steps:
1. Update to the latest version (v[X.X.X])
2. Restart your device
3. Ensure you have at least 500MB free storage
4. Reinstall the app

If crashes continue, please share:
- Device model and OS version
- When does it crash? (on launch, during playback, etc.)
- Any error messages?

This info will help our engineers investigate.

Best,
[Agent Name]
```
