# Kona - Financial Projections (3-5 Year)

## Executive Summary

This document outlines Kona's financial projections based on conservative, moderate, and aggressive growth scenarios. All projections use validated unit economics and industry benchmarks.

---

## Key Assumptions

### User Growth Rates
| Scenario | Monthly Growth | Annual Growth |
|----------|---------------|---------------|
| Conservative | 10% | 214% |
| Moderate | 15% | 435% |
| Aggressive | 20% | 792% |

### Revenue Assumptions
- **ARPU:** $2.50/month (starting), growing to $3.50 by Year 3
- **Conversion Rate:** 8% free-to-paid
- **Subscription Mix:** 40% monthly, 60% annual by Year 2

### Cost Assumptions
- **CAC:** $1.20 (decreasing 10% annually with organic growth)
- **Content Cost:** 25% of revenue
- **Infrastructure:** $5,000 base + $0.05/user/month
- **Support:** $0.10/user/month
- **Payment Processing:** 2.9% of revenue

## Appendix: Calculation Methodology

### LTV Calculation
```
LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin × (1 / Churn Rate)
LTV = $2.50 × 0.65 × (1 / 0.05)
LTV = $32.50
```

### Payback Period
```
Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin)
Payback = $1.20 / ($2.50 × 0.65)
Payback = 0.74 months
```

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
```
MRR = Active Users × ARPU × Paid Conversion Rate
```
